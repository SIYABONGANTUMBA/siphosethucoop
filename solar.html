Solar Power for Beginners

How does solar work?

Simple in 3 steps

  1. 01

    SUN hits PANEL

    Panel on roof catches sunlight. Even on cloudy days in Durban/Jozi, it works.

  2. 02

    PANEL makes DC POWER

    Inverter changes it to AC power — same as Eskom plug.

  3. 03

    POWER runs your HOME

    Lights, TV, phone, WiFi. Extra power goes to battery for night.
